Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What the app accesses

Danyze Backup uses the Google Drive permission "drive.file". It can only see and change files that the app itself created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in that Drive.

What the app does with data

The app uploads backup copies of the owner's own files, lists them, and removes old backups on a schedule. Nothing is shared, sold, or sent to any other person or service.

Who uses it

The app is used only by its owner. It does not collect information about anyone else.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security, "Third-party apps with account access".
